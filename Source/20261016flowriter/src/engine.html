Letter → strokes

Each letter is stored as an ordered list of strokes (lead-in, oval, downstroke, exit). Strokes join end to end, so a word is one continuous path cut at teaching points. Dots and crosses (i, j, t, x) come last, after the word is written. The pen lifts between words, and after capitals that don't join (B D F H I O P S T V W).

Strokes → beats

After a 4-beat count-in (the song's first 4 beats when you use your own track), each stroke owns a window of b beats, and each space between words is a rest of r beats with no stroke. The glowing dot travels the stroke at exactly that pace. In Practice, lift the pen or wander off the path and the music stops right there; put the pen back on the path and it carries on. In Test, the music keeps going no matter what. Rests play through on their own so there is time to move to the next word.

Valid trace

Ink is matched to the stroke whose window it fell in. A stroke is valid when ≥80% of the ink sits inside the margin, ≥70% of the stroke is covered, and ≥60% of the ink was on time.
